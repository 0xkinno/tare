## 1. Sponsor primitive

The Binance Web3 API stack read as one surface: RWA Data (token list, per-token dynamic data, market and asset
status), Market (klines), Trading (aggregated quotes and RFQ), Transaction (simulation), Wallet; plus the three
issuers' own on-chain unit sources. Tare combines the RWA reference, the on-chain unit factor, a venue quote and a
simulation into one number: shares delivered per dollar.

## 2. Observed constraint

One ticker, three units, and no trustworthy price.

- **Three wrappers per ticker.** All {{tickers}} target tickers have a bStocks, an Ondo and an xStocks token on BSC
  ({{wrappers}} wrappers, {{byIssuer.BSTOCKS}} / {{byIssuer.ONDO}} / {{byIssuer.XSTOCKS}}).
  Each answers "how many shares is one token" through a different mechanism:

  | Issuer | Where the unit lives | Read | Effect of a dividend |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | bStocks | the token itself, ERC-8056 scaled UI amount | `uiMultiplier()` (with scheduled `newUIMultiplier`, `effectiveAt`) | raw `balanceOf` is unchanged; one token is worth more shares |
  | Ondo | a separate oracle, `SyntheticSharesOracle` | `getSValue(token)` returns `(sValue, paused)` | raw `balanceOf` is unchanged; token price is share price times sValue |
  | xStocks | the token itself, rebasing | `getCurrentMultiplier()`; `balanceOf` is shares x multiplier, and a fee can lower the multiplier each period | `balanceOf` itself rises |

- **The units are not 1.** Today {{units.nonOneByIssuer.BSTOCKS}} of {{byIssuer.BSTOCKS}} bStocks and
  {{units.nonOneByIssuer.ONDO}} of {{byIssuer.ONDO}} Ondo names carry a unit different from one share per token, and
  {{units.nonOneByIssuer.XSTOCKS}} of {{byIssuer.XSTOCKS}} xStocks carry a multiplier different from one. The largest
  is {{units.maxUnitPct}} percent above one share per token, and the same ticker differs between its Ondo and
  bStocks tokens by up to {{units.maxOndoVsBstocksBps}} bps from unit alone. No unit is paused at that block
  (paused any: {{units.pausedAny}}). That one xStocks `balanceOf` unit equals one share follows the issuer's
  documentation; the xStocks pools are too thin to confirm it from price, so that mapping is UNKNOWN until a deeper
  venue or the keyed quotes confirm it.
- **The API's reference is not an independent price.**
  {{reference.bstocksWithoutStockPrice}} of {{reference.bstocksTotal}} bStocks have no equity reference price in the
  per-token dynamic response (`stockInfo.price` is null) and no market status there. For Ondo names, the documented
  derivation `tokenPrice / sharesMultiplier` agrees with the equity feed to within
  {{reference.ondoDerivedVsStockMaxAbsPct}} percent: it is the same feed times the unit, not a second opinion.
  For xStocks the API's token price is an on-chain print: it sits up to
  {{reference.xstocksApiPriceVsStockMaxAbsPct}} percent from the equity reference, and
  {{reference.xstocksApiPriceOver5pct}} of {{byIssuer.XSTOCKS}} are more than 5 percent away.
- **The API's xStocks multiplier does not match the token.** The list and dynamic endpoints agree with the chain for
  {{apiVsChain.bstocksMatch}} of {{apiVsChain.bstocksTotal}} bStocks and {{apiVsChain.ondoMatch}} of
  {{apiVsChain.ondoTotal}} Ondo names, but {{apiVsChain.xstocksMismatch}} of {{apiVsChain.xstocksTotal}} xStocks
  differ from the token's own multiplier, by up to {{apiVsChain.xstocksMaxMultDeviationBps}} bps.
- **Liquidity is not symmetric.** Median deepest pool: bStocks {{liquidity.BSTOCKS.medianDeepestPoolUsd}} USD, Ondo
  {{liquidity.ONDO.medianDeepestPoolUsd}} USD, xStocks {{liquidity.XSTOCKS.medianDeepestPoolUsd}} USD.
  {{liquidity.ONDO.under10k}} of {{liquidity.ONDO.n}} Ondo and {{liquidity.XSTOCKS.under10k}} of
  {{liquidity.XSTOCKS.n}} xStocks tokens have no pool above 10,000 USD.
- **Ondo names are RFQ-only in the aggregator.** Per the Trading API documentation, Ondo names are
  always RFQ (owner EIP-712 signature). bStocks have an AMM route (LiquidMesh) and an RFQ route; xStocks are AMM.
  Measured routing by the keyed endpoint is UNKNOWN until an API key is issued (E6).
- **The NYSE is closed {{weekendSharePct}} percent of the week** (hourly bins, holidays not modelled). In those hours
  every equity reference is hours or days old while tokens keep trading.

## 4. Why existing approaches do not solve it

A price monitor that differences token price and `referencePrice` compares numbers in different units: the unit is
up to {{units.maxOndoVsBstocksBps}} bps between two tokens of one ticker before any premium exists, and the
"reference" is the feed multiplied by the same unit for Ondo, absent for bStocks and a pool print for xStocks.
A guard that lives in the app protects only while the app is open and an owner is awake to sign, because the
aggregator's Ondo route needs the owner's signature. A vault that acts while the owner sleeps must take the
deposit. No unit is read in the transaction that enforces the limit.

At $50 on the direct on-chain pools, the cheapest-per-token pick and the most-shares pick coincide for
{{perShareRouting.tickersWithTwoRoutes}} of {{perShareRouting.tickersWithTwoRoutes}} tickers that have two routes
(naive differs from per-share: {{perShareRouting.naiveDiffersFromTare}}). The pick is not where the value is today. The
value is the guarantee, below.

## 5. New capability enabled

A **standing order in shares**: the owner signs once "buy this many dollars of NVDA, deliver at least this many
shares", and any untrusted keeper may fill it at any hour from whichever allowlisted wrapper and route delivers the
most shares. The contract reads the wrapper's unit itself in the same transaction and reverts below the floor.
No funds leave the wallet before the fill. Contracts can receive every wrapper tested
({{contractReceive.allOk}} of {{contractReceive.tested}} transfer simulations accepted; {{contractReceive.noHolder}}
wrappers had no holder to simulate from and stay UNKNOWN).

## 6. One security/business invariant

**T2, Share floor.** A fill never delivers fewer share-equivalents than `order.minShares`. Share-equivalents are
`tokensOut x unit`, with `unit` read on-chain inside the fill transaction (T3) from the source in section 2, never
passed by the keeper. Checked by: `TareBook.fill` revert `ShareFloor(got, min)`, fork tests, and the offline
verifier recomputing from logs.

## 7. One failure mode

A limit written in tokens. The unit moves without a transfer: bStocks validate a multiplier anywhere in
[1e-9, 1e9] (`_validateMultiplier`), Ondo allows large updates behind a scheduled pause, and xStocks rebases
`balanceOf`. An order "at least 0.2 tokens" signed before a 10 for 1 split or a pause would fill at a share count
that no longer matches the owner's intent. In shares the same order reverts with `ShareFloor` or `UnitPaused`.
Unit tests for this live in Phase 2 (`test_unitJump`); until then this item is a design claim, not a result.

## Thesis

Because tokenized stocks on BSC are wrapped by different issuers that each carry their own unit mechanism, against
an equity reference that is absent for bStocks and stale for {{weekendSharePct}} percent of the week, Tare can give a buyer the most real shares per dollar and make that
promise survive a unit change on-chain, for first-time savers who cannot stay awake to sign.
